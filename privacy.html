Information we collect

  • Payment details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay, our payment partner. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves.

How we use your information

  • To reserve your seat and send you the Google Meet link and joining details.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason.
